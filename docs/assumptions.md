# Operational Assumptions & Boundary Register

## 1. Architectural Boundary Principle

To maintain analytical integrity and avoid misleading stakeholders:
- **Observed Business Facts**: Transactions, sales volumes, historical prices, and product attributes observed in transactional logs.
- **Model Projections**: Out-of-sample statistical predictions and confidence intervals generated by mathematical models.
- **Assumed Operational Policies**: External configuration parameters governing lead times, target service levels, holding rates, and review cycles.
- **Scenario Stresses**: Synthetic perturbations (+15% demand, +20% lead time) executed to test supply chain fragility.

## 2. Assumptions Register

| Assumption | Assigned Value | Source | Analytical Necessity | Business Sensitivity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Cycle Service Level** | `95.0%` ($Z = 1.6449$) | Enterprise Policy Configuration | Dictates Safety Stock multiplier; represents probability of avoiding a stockout during replenishment cycle. | **High**: Moving from 95% to 99% increases Safety Stock capital by $+41.4\%$. |
| **Supplier Lead Time ($L$)** | `5 to 10 days` (by category) | Procurement Contract Guidelines | Determines expected demand accumulation between PO placement and dock arrival ($DDLT$). | **High**: A $+20\%$ lead time delay proportionally increases lead-time demand and buffer needs. |
| **Lead Time Variability ($\sigma_L$)** | `0.8 to 2.0 days` (std dev) | Inbound Freight Performance Benchmark | Accounts for port congestion, carrier transit variance, and customs hold-ups in safety stock. | **High**: Accounts for $25\%\text{--}40\%$ of total safety stock volume on high-velocity items. |
| **Periodic Review Period ($R$)** | `7 calendar days` | S&OP Operational Cadence | Establishes the Order-Up-To Level ($S = \bar{D}(L+R) + SS$) for weekly replenishment cycles. | **Medium**: Lengthening review intervals requires proportionally higher cycle stock. |
| **Annual Holding Cost Rate** | `25.0%` of unit cost per year | Corporate Finance WACC & Storage Standard | Quantifies working capital cost of carry, warehouse footprint, insurance, shrinkage, and obsolescence. | **Medium**: Dictates economic holding cost exposure on excess inventory. |
| **Daily Holding Cost Rate** | `0.0685%` per day | Calculated: $0.25 / 365$ | Used for daily inventory carrying cost calculations in SQL and Power BI. | **Low**: Linear scaling factor. |
| **Zero-Demand Semantic** | True Zero Demand | Data Engineering Pipeline Decision | Distinguishes between zero sales on an active SKU and an unobserved/dropped record. | **High**: Treating unobserved dates as missing rather than zero biases forecast upward by $+20\text{--}35\%$. |
| **Critical Stockout Horizon** | `< 3.0 days` of supply | Operations Threshold | Classifies urgent stockout exposure requiring immediate procurement escalation. | **High**: Directly drives the `P1 - Critical` priority bucket. |
| **Excess Stock Threshold** | `> 45.0 days` of supply | Merchandising Inventory Policy | Flags capital lockup exceeding normal replenishment safety ceilings. | **Medium**: Used to identify candidates for markdown or transfer. |
| **Dead Stock Horizon** | `60 consecutive days` zero demand | Catalog Lifecycle Rule | Identifies stagnant inventory with positive on-hand balances for liquidation. | **Low**: Affects discontinuation workflows. |
| **Fixed Order Cost ($S$)** | `$50.00` per PO | Procurement Cost Benchmark | Fixed administrative cost to issue, track, and receive a replenishment purchase order. | **Low**: Benchmark parameter. |
| **Stockout Penalty Multiple** | `2.0x` Gross Margin | Customer Lifetime Value Assumption | Models lost goodwill and customer churn penalty beyond immediate lost margin. | **Medium**: Used in lost margin opportunity models. |
